How it started

From an apprentice's roof to a small business.

Jacob Tyler spent three years at an established roofing company, learning the trade properly. By the end of it, he knew how he wanted to run his own.

He opened Tyler Roofing and Home Solutions in 2023, as a mobile, come-to-you roofing and home services company serving the Birmingham metro and central Alabama.

Today the company runs two to three crews across Shelby and Jefferson counties, with 25 years of combined roofing experience between them. Over 100 jobs since opening, held to the same standard he learned at the start.
