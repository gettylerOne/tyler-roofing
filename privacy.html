01

What we collect

The information collected depends on the form you use:

  • Contact form: name, email, optional phone number, and your message (the msg field).
  • Booking / inspection form: name, email, phone, street address, city, state, ZIP, project type, property, urgency, requested visit time, and notes. The roof flow also collects insurance / claim information. Submissions include the selected flow and an automatically generated subject.
  • Spam prevention: the contact and booking forms include a hidden bot-field honeypot field, intended to be left empty.

The short “Schedule a Free Inspection” forms ask for name, phone, ZIP, and “what do you need?” They only prefill the booking modal; they do not themselves submit to Netlify. You send the request when you submit the booking form.

02

Why we collect it

We use your information to respond to your questions, schedule inspections, provide estimates, and follow up on your project. When you ask us to, we also use it to coordinate with your insurance carrier.

03

Who we share it with

We use these providers to handle your request:

  • Netlify: hosts the website and processes forms. Submissions are stored in Netlify Forms, and Netlify can email form notifications to the business inbox.
  • Flint Systems: receives form submissions forwarded by our Netlify submission-created function so we can manage requests on our lead board.
  • Email: we may email about your request through our email provider or business inbox, tylerroofingandhomesolutions@gmail.com.

We may share relevant project information with your insurance carrier when you ask us to coordinate with them. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or third-party marketers.

04

Cookies & tracking

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and how people use the site, such as which pages they view and their device type. Analytics may use cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

05

Calls & texts

We may call or text about your own request, including confirming a requested visit or discussing scheduling. This is follow-up about your project, not a marketing SMS subscription. Tell us by phone or email if you want us to stop contacting you.
